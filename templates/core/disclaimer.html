Educational Disclaimer

Last Updated: October 2026
Independent Student Resource Notice:
Pariksha is an independent student academic resource platform. It is not officially operated, sponsored, or endorsed by Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV) or any affiliated university body. All university names, branch codes, and curriculum syllabi are referenced solely for factual academic identification under fair use principles.
1. Practice Papers vs. Real Exams

Any future generated "Predicted Practice Paper" or "Must Prepare Topics" feature is an educational practice instrument designed to simulate exam blueprints for revision. It does NOT constitute, represent, or substitute for an actual, official, or leaked examination paper.

2. Accuracy of Historical Records

While student reviewers check contributed question papers against official scheme codes, students are encouraged to cross-reference with official university notifications regarding scheme revisions, course codes, and passing criteria.

3. Non-AI and Future AI Separation

Pariksha currently provides verified document archival, catalog filtering, and peer review workflows. AI assistance and automated topic weightage features are clearly marked as pending future model integration and are not currently active in production.
